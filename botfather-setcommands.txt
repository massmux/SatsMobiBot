# Instructions:

1 - Talk to the botfather
2 - Use: /setcommands
3 - Select your tip bot
4 - Paste this:

help - Read the help.
balance - Check balances. 
transactions - Transactions list.
tip - Reply to a message to tip: /tip 50
send - Send Hot funds to a user: /send 100 @SatsMobiBot
invoice - Create Lightning invoice: /invoice 1000
pay - Pay Invoice: /pay lnbc10n1ps...
donate - Donate: /donate 1000
faucet - Create a faucet: /faucet 2100 21 
pos - Create POS
swaptosafe - Swap ALL Hot to Safe: /swaptosafe
swaptohot - Fill Hot from Safe up to max: /swaptohot
swapall - Swap ALL from Hot to Safe (alias): /swapall
